🎯 Mistakes Made & Corrections

Key points noticed in today's speaking practice with Italian contrastive grammar notes.

Mistake Review Telling Time
❌ Avoid / What was said
"at the five and thirty" / "five and thirty"
✅ Natural English
five thirty / half past five
🇮🇹
Le cinque e mezza (17:30 / 05:30)
In English we never use an article before time (no "at the"). Use the digital format five thirty or the conversational format half past five.
"Our call is scheduled for five thirty tomorrow afternoon." 🔊
La nostra chiamata è programmata per le cinque e mezza domani pomeriggio.
"I usually leave the office at half past five." 🔊
Di solito esco dall'ufficio alle cinque e mezza.
Collocation Travel & Geography
❌ Literal Italian habit
"cross the sea" (when meaning traveling abroad)
✅ Natural Usage
cross the sea / travel overseas / go abroad
🇮🇹
Attraversare il mare / Viaggiare oltreoceano / Andare all'estero
"Cross the sea" is literal (e.g., in a boat or ferry). When you mean traveling to another country or expanding international business, native speakers say go overseas, travel abroad, or cross the ocean/Atlantic.
"He decided to move overseas to take his career to the next level." 🔊
Ha deciso di trasferirsi all'estero per far fare un salto di qualità alla sua carriera.
"The ferry took two hours to cross the sea to the island." 🔊
Il traghetto ha impiegato due ore per attraversare il mare fino all'isola.
Sport Vocab Collocation
⚠️ Context Difference
"football society" / confusing team vs club
✅ Natural English
football club (FC) / football team
🇮🇹
Club di calcio / Società calcistica
Never say "football society" (false friend from società calcistica). Use football club (e.g., AC Milan Football Club) for the organization, and football team for the group of 11 players.
"He manages a prestigious football club in Northern Italy." 🔊
Dirige un prestigioso club di calcio nel nord Italia.
Sport Terminology
❌ Literal Translation
"attacker" / "offensive player"
✅ Accurate English Term
striker / centre-forward
🇮🇹
Attaccante / Punta centrale / Centravanti
While "attacker" or "forward" is a generic category, the dedicated goal scorer playing upfront is specifically called a striker.
"The team urgently needs a clinical striker who can score 20 goals a season." 🔊
La squadra ha urgente bisogno di un attaccante cinico che possa segnare 20 gol a stagione.
Phrasal Verb Grammar Rule
❌ Pronoun Placement Trap
"you took over it" / "you take control of it"
✅ Correct Separable Order
you took it over
🇮🇹
L'hai rilevato / Ne hai preso il controllo / gestione
Golden Grammar Rule: "Take over" is a separable phrasal verb. When the object is a pronoun (it / them), it must go in the middle: "You took IT over" (never "took over it").
"When the founder stepped down, you took it over and doubled revenue." 🔊
Quando il fondatore si è dimesso, l'hai rilevata e hai raddoppiato il fatturato.
Pronunciation & Form Adjective
❌ Common Pitfalls
"not so empatic" / "not sensitive"
✅ Standard English Form
not so empathetic /ˌem.pəˈθet.ɪk/
🇮🇹
Non così empatico / Poco empatico
Stress the third syllable: em-puh-THET-ik. Be careful not to confuse empathetic (capable of understanding others' feelings) with sensitive (sensibile / permaloso).
"His response was logical, but he came across as not so empathetic." 🔊
La sua risposta era logica, ma è sembrato poco empatico.
Verb Form Useful Verb
❌ Clumsy phrasing
"make more bad" / "do it worse"
✅ Natural Verbs
worsen / get worse
🇮🇹
Peggiorare / Aggravarsi
You can use worsen (more formal) or get worse (conversational). Example: "Conditions worsened" or "Conditions got worse".
"Ignoring the client's complaint will only worsen the situation." 🔊
Ignorare il reclamo del cliente non farà che peggiorare la situazione.
Common Italian Error Comparative Rule
❌ Classic Trap (Molto Meglio)
"more better" / "very better"
✅ Correct English
much better (or: far better)
🇮🇹
Molto meglio
Never say "more better"! Because better is already comparative, you modify it with much or far, never with "more" or "very".
"Your explanation today was much better and far more concise." 🔊
La tua spiegazione oggi è stata molto meglio e molto più concisa.
Ending -ed Idiom
❌ Missing ending
"first come first serve" (missing the -ed)
✅ Proper Idiom
first come, first served
🇮🇹
Chi prima arriva meglio alloggia / In ordine di arrivo
Notice the -ed at the end of served: literally, "the first who comes is the first who is served".
"Conference seats are allocated on a first come, first served basis." 🔊
I posti per la conferenza vengono assegnati in ordine di arrivo.

✨ New Vocabulary & Idioms

Fresh words and idiomatic expressions from our discussion today.

Adjective C1 Advanced
unforeseen /ˌʌn.fɔːˈsiːn/
🇮🇹
Imprevisto / Inaspettato
Very frequently used in professional settings: "unforeseen circumstances" (circostanze impreviste) or "unforeseen expenses".
"Due to unforeseen events, the project deadline had to be extended." 🔊
A causa di eventi imprevisti, la scadenza del progetto è stata prorogata.
Collocation Lifestyle
no balance
🇮🇹
Mancanza di equilibrio / Squilibrio
Most often used when talking about work vs. personal life (work-life balance): "He has no balance in his life".
"When you work twelve hours every day, there is no balance." 🔊
Quando lavori dodici ore al giorno, non c'è alcun equilibrio.
Collocation Business & Ethics
no loyalty
🇮🇹
Nessuna lealtà / Slealtà / Mancanza di fedeltà
Refers to lack of commitment to employees, an employer, or partners: "to show no loyalty".
"Many workers left the company because management showed no loyalty to them." 🔊
Molti lavoratori hanno lasciato l'azienda perché la direzione non ha mostrato alcuna lealtà verso di loro.
Idiom Praise / Excellence
above and beyond
🇮🇹
Oltre le aspettative / Fare molto più del proprio dovere
Often used with the verb go: to go above and beyond (fare il passo in più, superare ogni aspettativa).
"He went above and beyond to deliver the project ahead of time." 🔊
Ha fatto molto più del suo dovere per consegnare il progetto in anticipo.
Famous Idiom Emotional Intelligence
don't wear your heart on your sleeve
🇮🇹
Non mostrare apertamente le tue emozioni / Non essere un libro aperto
Literal meaning: non portare il cuore sulla manica della camicia. Idiomatic meaning: keep your feelings guarded; don't make your emotional vulnerabilities obvious to everyone.
"In tough negotiations, don't wear your heart on your sleeve." 🔊
Nelle trattative difficili, non mostrare apertamente cosa provi; resta impassibile.
Adjective Personality
determined /dɪˈtɜː.mɪnd/
🇮🇹
Determinato / Risoluto / Tenace
Pronunciation check: don't pronounce the "-ed" as an extra syllable ("de-ter-min-ed" ❌); it's two syllables: de-TER-mind ✅.
"Mauro is extremely determined to speak fluent English." 🔊
Mauro è estremamente determinato a parlare un inglese fluente.
Motto / Proverb Mindset
who dares wins
🇮🇹
Chi osa vince / La fortuna aiuta gli audaci
Famous motto (originally used by the British Special Air Service - SAS). Encourages boldness and taking courageous calculated risks.
"It was a risky move to launch the new brand, but who dares wins!" 🔊
È stata una mossa rischiosa lanciare il nuovo marchio, ma chi osa vince!
Noun Education / Merit
scholarship (you win)
🇮🇹
Borsa di studio per merito
Key Distinction: A scholarship is won through merit, outstanding academic grades, or athletic ability. Collocation: "to win / earn a scholarship".
"He won a prestigious scholarship to study at Cambridge." 🔊
Ha vinto una prestigiosa borsa di studio per merito per studiare a Cambridge.
Noun Financial Aid
bursary / grant (free / need-based)
🇮🇹
Sussidio economico / Contributo a fondo perduto / Borsa di studio per reddito
Difference from scholarship: A bursary or grant is awarded primarily based on financial need or social criteria to help cover tuition/living costs (non-repayable).
"Students from low-income families can apply for a state bursary." 🔊
Gli studenti provenienti da famiglie a basso reddito possono richiedere un sussidio statale.
Idiom / Phrase
first come, first served
🇮🇹
Chi prima arriva meglio alloggia / Assegnazione in ordine di arrivo
Very common for tickets, hotel deals, limited course registrations, and special business offers.
"Parking spaces are available on a first come, first served basis." 🔊
I posti auto sono disponibili in ordine di arrivo.
Irregular Past Verb Workplace & Rights
hid the pregnancies (hide → hid → hidden)
🇮🇹
Ha nascosto le gravidanze / Celare la maternità
Remember the irregular verb forms: hide (present) → hid (past simple) → hidden (past participle). Plural: pregnancy → pregnancies.
"Due to strict contract penalties, she hid her pregnancies from the agency." 🔊
A causa di severe penali contrattuali, ha nascosto le sue gravidanze all'agenzia.
