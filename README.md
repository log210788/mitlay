## 📌 What's Included

### 1. 🎯 Common Mistakes & Contrastive Grammar (Italian &rarr; English)
Detailed explanations on why these mistakes happen and how native speakers phrase them:
- **five thirty / half past five** (Avoiding "at the five and thirty" – rules for saying time)
- **cross the sea / travel overseas / go abroad** (Natural collocations vs literal Italian)
- **football club (FC) vs football team** (Avoiding "football society")
- **striker / centre-forward** (Football position precision vs "attacker")
- **you took it over** (Separable phrasal verb: object pronoun in the middle)
- **not so empathetic** (Pronunciation `/ˌem.pəˈθet.ɪk/` & usage vs *sensitive*)
- **worsen / get worse** (Avoiding clumsy phrasing like "make more bad")
- **much better / far better** (Never "more better" or "very better")
- **first come, first served** (Remembering the final *-ed*)

### 2. ✨ New Vocabulary & Idioms
Complete with Italian definitions, register, pronunciation guides, and real-world example sentences:
- **unforeseen** (*imprevisto / inaspettato*)
- **no balance** (*mancanza di equilibrio / squilibrio*)
- **no loyalty** (*nessuna lealtà / slealtà*)
- **above and beyond** (*andare oltre le aspettative / fare più del dovuto*)
- **don't wear your heart on your sleeve** (*non mostrare apertamente le tue emozioni*)
- **determined** (*determinato / tenace*)
- **who dares wins** (*chi osa vince*)
- **scholarship** (merit-based – *borsa di studio per merito*)
- **bursary / grant** (need-based – *sussidio economico a fondo perduto*)
- **first come, first served** (*chi prima arriva meglio alloggia*)
- **hid the pregnancies** (irregular past: *hide &rarr; hid &rarr; hidden*)

### 3. 🎧 Curated Listening & Real-world Practice
- **BBC 6 Minute English**: *"Is your job taking over your life?"*
- **BBC The English We Speak**: *"Wear your heart on your sleeve"*
- **TED Talk**: *"Grit: The power of passion and perseverance"* (Angela Duckworth)

### 4. ⚡ Interactive Study Features
- **Native Audio Pronunciation**: Built-in speech synthesis (Web Speech API) to hear any word or sentence aloud in British/US English.
- **Interactive Flashcards Mode**: Flip between English and Italian for active recall.
- **Instant Search**: Filter by Italian or English words, grammar tags, or example sentences.
- **Dark / Light Theme Toggle**: Seamless readability day or night.
- **Print & PDF Support**: Formatted for clean printing or saving to PDF.
